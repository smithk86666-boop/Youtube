# Niche selection: sleep vs meditation vs motivation (researched 2026-10-26)

**Decision:** sleep (narrated sleep stories / calm long-form narration for adults). Meditation is the adjacent fallback. Motivation is deprioritized.

## Estimated figures (third-party, directional only)

| Niche | Est. RPM | Competition | Watch time per viewer | Policy risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sleep / healing sounds | ~$5-11 | Moderate; music-only is saturated | Very high (plays through the night) | Lower with original scripts |
| Meditation / ambience | ~$4-10 | Moderate | High | Lower |
| Motivation | ~$6-12 | Very high | Low (short videos) | Highest (templated, reused footage) |

Across 98 audited faceless niches one source reports a mean RPM near $11 and median near $10, with finance leading. Sleep RPM is therefore roughly average; the case for sleep is watch hours per viewer and evergreen reuse, not rate.

## Reasoning

- Goal is watch time first, then money. Hours per viewer is the main lever, and it directly serves the YPP watch-hour threshold.
- Evergreen: one video can earn for years.
- Cheap to produce with a script, a calm voice, and a looping visual.
- Lowest inauthentic-content exposure when scripts are original.

## Caveats

- Sleep-story-specific revenue data was not found. A claim of a bedtime-story channel earning ~$500K/year had no verifiable source and is excluded.
- Month-12 income projections around $1-5K/month are unverified.
- Sub-niche demand is a hypothesis until sampled directly on YouTube.
