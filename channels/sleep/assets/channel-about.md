# Channel About text (drafts)

Working channel name: Quiet Hours. Pick one variant for the voice.

## Variant A: synthetic voice

Quiet Hours is a channel of slow, calm history made for winding down at the end of the day. Each video is a single gentle telling of one place and one time, with a quiet sound bed and no sudden noises.

Every script is written for this channel from several sources, checked against them, and listed in the video description so you can read further. Narration uses a synthetic voice. We say so because you should know.

These videos are for relaxation and entertainment. They are not medical advice and do not treat any condition. If you struggle with sleep or worry about your health, please speak with a qualified professional.

New videos twice a week.

Quiet Hours is a channel of slow, calm history made for winding down at the end of the day. Each video is a single gentle telling of one place and one time, read by a human narrator over a quiet sound bed.
