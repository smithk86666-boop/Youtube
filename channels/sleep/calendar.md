# Quiet Hours: first 90 days

Cadence: 2 videos per week once production runs. Start date is whenever the human setup steps in `docs/runbook-human-steps.md` are done; weeks are relative.

| Weeks | Goal | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup and first upload | Runbook steps done: account, channel, voice plan, AdSense link. Script 001 is ready; produce and upload it |
| 2-3 | Establish rhythm | Publish 002-005. Run `niche-sampling` and adjust topics. Check Content ID on the bed |
| 4-6 | Core series | Publish 006-010. Test length (60 vs 90 vs 120) and mid-roll |
| 7 | First review | `weekly-review` with 28-day data on 001-004. Decide on length, titles, thumbnail style |
| 8-10 | Probes | Add `still-weather` (2 videos, needs provenance) and `gentle-stories` (2 videos). Keep core cadence |
| 11-13 | Decide and push | Compare series per `docs/experiments/2026-q4-series-test.md`. Concentrate on what works. Check YPP progress against thresholds in force |

## Weekly routine
- Mon: choose topics, outline, research
- Tue-Wed: write and fact-check scripts
- Thu: narration, render, compliance gate
- Fri: upload, schedule, `weekly-review` of last week
