# Quiet Hours strategy

## Positioning
Calm, accurate bedtime history for adults. Demand is proven (the leading channel in this space has about 702K subscribers and ~130K average views per video, per aggregator data). The space is also flooded with inaccurate, templated AI videos that YouTube says it is cracking down on. We win on trust: right facts, listed sources, distinct videos, consistent voice.

## What we copy from the top performers
- Evergreen, searchable historical settings and "what was it like" angles.
- Long videos made for overnight playback and autoplay.
- A recognizable title formula and voice, used consistently.
- A steady cadence.
- Income beyond ads over time (the largest meditation creator also sells courses and products).

## What we refuse to copy
- Competitor title prefixes and brand wording.
- Mass-produced uploads with minimal variation, glitchy audio, anachronistic visuals, shaky facts.
- Compilations padded with filler to hit a length.

## Audience and search intent
Queries to sample (validate with `niche-sampling` skill): "history for sleep", "medieval history to fall asleep", "bedtime history", "relaxing history documentary no music", "ancient Rome sleep", "daily life in [era] for sleep".

## Format
- Series `quiet-history`: 60-120 min, one narrator, soft rain or wind bed generated by us, dark illustrated or photographic still per video that is accurate for the topic.
- Probes later: `still-weather`, `gentle-stories`.

## Monetization plan
1. YPP ads. Thresholds: 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours (reported to rise to 8,000 hours on 2027-02-01 for new applicants; verify). At long durations, 4,000 hours is about 2,700 viewers at 90 minutes of average watch time each, or fewer if sessions run long. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.
2. Affiliate links in descriptions (sleep and white-noise products, sleep apps), with required disclosures.
3. Sponsorships once average views justify outreach.
4. Longer term: an audio-only podcast feed of the same scripts, only if rights and policy allow.
