# Quiet Hours strategy

## Positioning
Calm, factual bedtime narration for adults: the interesting-but-gentle history and science you can drift off to. The promise is a pleasant voice and a topic that holds no tension, not a cure for sleeplessness.

Sub-niche is a hypothesis. Alternatives to test if validation fails: guided sleep meditation, fiction sleep stories, nature-and-voice hybrids.

## Why this niche (see `docs/research/2026-10-niche-selection.md`)
- Highest watch hours per viewer among the three candidates.
- Evergreen catalog; low production cost.
- Lowest policy exposure when scripts are original.
- RPM is about average, so volume of watch time must carry revenue.

## Audience and search intent
Probable queries: "history for sleep", "bedtime story for adults", "relaxing documentary to fall asleep", "calm science facts to sleep". Not yet measured. Validation step below fills this in.

## Format
- 150 minutes default, tested against 60 and 480.
- One narrator voice, consistent across videos.
- Static or slowly looping dark visual, no flashing or bright changes.
- Quiet ambient bed under the voice; narration-led so music claims matter less.

## Monetization plan
1. YPP ads first. Thresholds: 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours (reported to rise to 8,000 hours on 2027-02-01 for new applicants; verify).
2. Affiliate links in descriptions after approval or earlier where allowed (sleep products, white noise, apps). Verify programs and disclosure rules.
3. Sponsorships once average views justify outreach.

## Validation (do before producing at scale)
- [ ] Sample top 10 results for 15 target queries. Record views, upload age, channel size, video length.
- [ ] Find 3 gaps: topics, voices, or lengths competitors underserve.
- [ ] Check the handle "Quiet Hours" and variants; pick the final name.
- [ ] Choose voice provider, confirm commercial license, audition 3 voices.
- [ ] Choose music source and confirm license; test one upload for Content ID claims.
- [ ] Publish the first 5 videos, review retention curves, then decide to scale or pivot.

## Risks
See `docs/research/2026-10-launch-risks.md`: API upload lock, YPP threshold increase, inauthentic-content enforcement, AI disclosure, licensing, mid-roll effect on sleepers, health claims.
