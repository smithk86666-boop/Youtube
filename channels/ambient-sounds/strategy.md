# Still Weather strategy

## Positioning
Real recordings of weather and water from real places, long enough to run all night. Differentiation is provenance: each video names where and when it was recorded.

## Why test it
- Lowest production cost of any option: no script, no narrator, no voice license.
- Viewers play long videos while sleeping, so watch hours per view can be high.
- It is also the most crowded and the highest-risk format for YPP rejection (see `CLAUDE.md` in this folder and `docs/research/2026-10-ambient-sounds.md`). That is why it is run as a test, not a bet.

## Audience and search intent
Long-tail queries that include a duration and a sound: "8 hours rain on window", "ocean waves for sleep", "wind in pine trees". Unmeasured; validation fills this in.

## Format
- 480 minutes default; test 180 and 600.
- Static photo or slow footage of the actual place, dark enough for a bedroom.
- Single sound source per video, no music.

## Monetization plan
1. YPP ads (same thresholds as the other channels; verify current numbers).
2. Affiliate links for sleep and white-noise products, after validation.
3. Small channels in this format are reported to earn very little (one aggregator shows about $0-3 per day at ~275 subscribers and ~615K views). Treat income as slow.

## Validation
- [ ] Sample top 10 results for 15 queries; record views, age, channel size, length, whether the visual is unique
- [ ] Decide recording method: own recorder (budget and learning time) or licensed library
- [ ] Record or license 5 distinct sources before publishing
- [ ] Publish the first 5; review retention, CTR, and any monetization-review signals

## Risks
Repetitive/inauthentic-content flagging, sound licensing, Content ID on library audio, mid-roll ads waking sleepers. See `docs/research/`.
